# Landing pages

How to design marketing and SaaS landing pages: product visuals, hero sections, copy, section layouts, social proof, pricing and footers.

Each rule cites its source as `author/video timestamp`; the link opens the video at that moment. Full source details are in `references/bibliography.md`.

## Product visuals and imagery

- Show the real product UI (actual dashboard, lightly edited screenshots) instead of stock images or generic feature icons; never bury what the product is in 12px text. Unrelated stock throws the page off; software otherwise reads like a wellness retreat ad. ([jain/captivating-sections 1:23](https://youtu.be/tNMAFjzapOk?t=83), [jain/landing-page-levels 0:44](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=44), [jain/saas-vibe-code-mistakes 5:56](https://youtu.be/PDcQJOPby1k?t=356))
- Invest real design effort in landing pages and focus on presentation, not complexity. This is where vibe-coded products lose most customers; better presentation means better conversion. ([jain/saas-vibe-code-mistakes 5:36](https://youtu.be/PDcQJOPby1k?t=336))
- Crop and zoom screenshots to the important parts, or better, craft visuals that show exactly what the product does. Curates what visitors see. ([jain/landing-page-levels 4:59](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=299))
- Add graphics to the page, even simple skewed product cards. Graphics are what elevate the page most. ([jain/saas-vibe-code-mistakes 5:48](https://youtu.be/PDcQJOPby1k?t=348))
- Use a high-quality hero image asset. A high-quality asset makes or breaks most landing pages. ([jain/learn-ui-in-10-minutes 1:20](https://youtu.be/9WVt1CelBfg?t=80))
- Use imagery that visibly relates to the stated audience's world. Builds trust; a page with no images feels robotic and vibe-coded. ([jain/vibe-coding-cant-fix 0:58](https://youtu.be/RCneB_MQ7qs?t=58))
- Show images of products or options so users can tell unfamiliar names apart. Like picture menus, visuals explain options without knowing names. ([jain/slept-on-trends 7:51](https://youtu.be/EHwZzWd-OnQ?t=471))
- Pick images for the layout's aspect ratio and expect heavy cropping; for cutout-text layouts, keep nothing important in the cut-off corner. That area will get cut off. ([jain/website-layouts 2:13](https://youtu.be/P2ksReDwWkE?t=133))
- Use large high-resolution photos whenever images are blown up big. Avoids pixelation. ([jain/website-layouts 3:32](https://youtu.be/P2ksReDwWkE?t=212))
- For skincare/beauty sites, use great product photos, bright white backgrounds and lots of white space. ([jain/skincare-redesign 0:00](https://youtu.be/BvbFPzLjWcU?t=0))

## Hero sections

- Differentiate the standard hero with a distinctive font, inline diagram, or interactive/animated product image such as the AI thinking state. Everyone uses the same layout; a live product animation is eye-catching. ([jain/google-ai-redesign 1:57](https://youtu.be/pGYLZyBE32o?t=117), [jain/modern-layouts 2:45](https://youtu.be/VPeTgU7la34?t=165))
- Keep contextual illustrations around a centered core message instead of stripping them for clarity. They explain the product without reading; removing them just feels empty. ([jain/boring-design-hacks 0:27](https://youtu.be/SfX43uIubj4?t=27))
- Build software heroes with big text, a big image, and lots of space. Users start scrolling for more information without realizing it. ([jain/modern-layouts 2:37](https://youtu.be/VPeTgU7la34?t=157))
- Keep the hero text block from dominating the page; give it intentional spacing. ([jain/learn-ui-in-10-minutes 0:41](https://youtu.be/9WVt1CelBfg?t=41))
- Prefer a stacked hero (text above visual) over side-by-side text and image. More modern and lets the site breathe. ([jain/landing-page-levels 2:28](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=148))
- Pick the hero layout from a few standard options: text left/image right, text over full-screen image, or text above image. Only a handful of layouts exist; contrast with the stacked-hero preference when modernizing a side-by-side hero. ([jain/learn-ui-in-10-minutes 3:23](https://youtu.be/9WVt1CelBfg?t=203))
- Never place text or UI over the image's focal point; put it above, below, or beside it. Leaves the imagery open. ([jain/learn-ui-in-10-minutes 1:26](https://youtu.be/9WVt1CelBfg?t=86))
- Choose a zoomed-out image for full-screen heroes so the subject leaves room for overlays. An oversized subject punches you in the face and collides with overlays. ([jain/tesla-wannabe-redesign 2:49](https://youtu.be/V3Omp1hm0Sg?t=169))
- Arrange 'scattered' hero images deliberately: larger elements below, smaller on top, with a safe margin around the text. It should feel random but actually isn't. ([jain/vibe-coding-cant-fix 5:54](https://youtu.be/RCneB_MQ7qs?t=354))
- Use 3D elements as a hero focal point; little other design is then needed. People are so enamored by 3D you don't need much else. ([jain/no-code-3d-website 1:04](https://youtu.be/A_Ozpb0XDuw?t=64))
- Pair the hero headline with subtext plus a primary and a secondary CTA. ([jain/skincare-redesign 5:08](https://youtu.be/BvbFPzLjWcU?t=308))
- Give the nav CTA and the hero CTA the same label. Implies both go to the same place, building the correct mental model. ([jain/landing-page-levels 3:28](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=208))
- Consider a type-in prompt or search bar as the hero CTA instead of a plain button. The user is already engaged in an action and more likely to finish it. ([jain/google-ai-redesign 1:08](https://youtu.be/pGYLZyBE32o?t=68))
- Add a prominent CTA section near the top when the header has no CTA. ([jain/tesla-wannabe-redesign 3:51](https://youtu.be/V3Omp1hm0Sg?t=231))

## Copy

- Write a single actionable hero headline (~7 words) promising exactly what the software does, with ~14-word subtext that directly supports it. Communicates the message as quickly as possible. ([jain/landing-page-levels 3:04](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=184), [jain/learn-ui-in-10-minutes 1:04](https://youtu.be/9WVt1CelBfg?t=64), [jain/vibe-coding-cant-fix 1:23](https://youtu.be/RCneB_MQ7qs?t=83))
- Cut vague, long feature copy drastically (e.g. 90%) to short lines and make the visuals bigger to carry the narrative. No one stops to read a block of text. ([jain/landing-page-levels 1:07](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=67), [jain/vibe-coding-cant-fix 3:49](https://youtu.be/RCneB_MQ7qs?t=229))
- Don't spend copy on what's already visible: no company name next to the logo, no labels like 'Financial Dashboard'. It's redundant; it should already be apparent. ([jain/finance-dashboard-redesign 1:09](https://youtu.be/Yr2uIcFZDDQ?t=69), [jain/landing-page-levels 1:17](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=77))
- Prefer blocks of text, simple illustrations and bold numbers over split text, pop-ups, ornaments and complex shapes. Easier to follow. ([jain/captivating-sections 1:02](https://youtu.be/tNMAFjzapOk?t=62))
- Write feature copy about how the product helps, not just what it does. Benefit-focused copy is much more valuable. ([jain/landing-page-levels 7:54](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=474))
- Paraphrase long specs and link to a full specs page rather than hiding them in accordion tabs. People won't read accordion tabs; they prefer looking at pictures. ([jain/tesla-wannabe-redesign 5:07](https://youtu.be/V3Omp1hm0Sg?t=307))
- Write friendly, natural copy instead of corporate jargon, e.g. 'we sweat the details'. ([jain/boring-design-hacks 5:34](https://youtu.be/SfX43uIubj4?t=334))
- Make CTA button labels actionable. ([jain/crucial-ui-elements 5:34](https://youtu.be/gKM6b2EnW1k?t=334))

## Page structure and section layouts

- Vary section layouts; mix full-screen sections, cards and three-column sections instead of repeating image-left/text-right. Boring websites repeat one column layout over and over. ([jain/awful-to-amazing 0:54](https://youtu.be/ulSOdTgoGeY?t=54), [jain/tesla-wannabe-redesign 4:48](https://youtu.be/V3Omp1hm0Sg?t=288))
- Use simple bento grids on plain grid layouts when many features would run off screen. Nothing is lost while scrolling; complex Apple-style bentos are hard to build. ([jain/learn-ui-in-10-minutes 4:24](https://youtu.be/9WVt1CelBfg?t=264), [jain/modern-layouts 5:44](https://youtu.be/VPeTgU7la34?t=344))
- Build a logical top-to-bottom flow with smooth transitions, not sections dropped on top of each other. Abrupt sections feel disconnected; good flow encourages scrolling. ([jain/landing-page-levels 3:49](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=229))
- Keep pages no longer than needed; tighten sections so they feel cohesive. ([jain/tesla-wannabe-redesign 1:10](https://youtu.be/V3Omp1hm0Sg?t=70))
- Merge redundant landing pages selling the same thing into one built on their few key selling points. ([jain/google-ai-redesign 4:02](https://youtu.be/pGYLZyBE32o?t=242))
- Stack two-column feature layouts into one vertical column with large elements. Focuses the user on one feature at a time. ([jain/awful-to-amazing 3:19](https://youtu.be/ulSOdTgoGeY?t=199))
- Avoid stacking full-screen images section after section on product sites. It's a lazy pattern. ([jain/tesla-wannabe-redesign 1:32](https://youtu.be/V3Omp1hm0Sg?t=92))
- Use stacked, sliding-up cards to present 3 to 5 options in good detail. ([jain/website-layouts 6:21](https://youtu.be/P2ksReDwWkE?t=381))
- Use a thin cropped photo with profound text as an interlude between text-heavy sections. Acts as a spacer. ([jain/website-layouts 1:31](https://youtu.be/P2ksReDwWkE?t=91))
- Put an overlay and text on wide landscape shots for brand-messaging sections. Creates an impactful brand moment. ([jain/awful-to-amazing 2:38](https://youtu.be/ulSOdTgoGeY?t=158))
- For a profound statement, center the text, enlarge it, and give it lots of space. ([jain/learn-ui-in-10-minutes 4:11](https://youtu.be/9WVt1CelBfg?t=251))
- Use hover pop-out images on key words to illustrate content without extra text or space. ([jain/micro-animations 5:12](https://youtu.be/ld1zhQMXxXU?t=312))

## Social proof and trust

- Include a row of well-recognized customer logos on software landing pages. 41 of 50 sites did; users assume logos are important companies using it. ([jain/landing-page-levels 5:54](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=354), [jain/modern-layouts 0:38](https://youtu.be/VPeTgU7la34?t=38))
- Add extra social proof beyond the logo ribbon: testimonials, badges, and a link to a social-proof page. Strengthens the brand. ([jain/landing-page-levels 5:54](https://youtu.be/eMMiLeo_UGI?t=354), [jain/vibe-coding-cant-fix 3:57](https://youtu.be/RCneB_MQ7qs?t=237))
- For a simple logo bar, put only your most important logos in one row across the screen. ([jain/modern-layouts 1:08](https://youtu.be/VPeTgU7la34?t=68))
- For a fancier logo bar, use a marquee with edge gradients and progressive blur fading logos out. ([jain/modern-layouts 1:16](https://youtu.be/VPeTgU7la34?t=76))
- Don't use a written testimonial in the header as social proof. ([jain/skincare-redesign 0:56](https://youtu.be/BvbFPzLjWcU?t=56))
- Include a privacy/security section for any software or hardware handling user data. Users need privacy reassurance. ([jain/google-ai-redesign 5:32](https://youtu.be/pGYLZyBE32o?t=332))

## Pricing and footers

- Give each pricing plan a name and a short description. Makes the plan easier to identify. ([jain/crucial-ui-elements 5:02](https://youtu.be/gKM6b2EnW1k?t=302))
- List plan features with check marks instead of bullets, and include a feature the plan doesn't get. ([jain/crucial-ui-elements 4:55](https://youtu.be/gKM6b2EnW1k?t=295))
- Add a note under the price disclosing billing terms, e.g. the billed monthly total. So users don't think you're bad people when they find the real price. ([jain/crucial-ui-elements 5:14](https://youtu.be/gKM6b2EnW1k?t=314))
- Separate price from features with a reduced-opacity primary-color background instead of a divider line. ([jain/crucial-ui-elements 5:22](https://youtu.be/gKM6b2EnW1k?t=322))
- Name the top high-volume tier 'Enterprise'. Users needing that volume probably need more than a fixed plan. ([jain/saas-vibe-code-mistakes 4:19](https://youtu.be/PDcQJOPby1k?t=259))
- Make the purchase CTA one of the cards in the group (e.g. gradient stroke) rather than a separate pop-up area. Keeps the section more interesting. ([jain/google-ai-redesign 4:23](https://youtu.be/pGYLZyBE32o?t=263))
- For 3-5 footer links, center them with logo above, socials below, copyright left and credits right. Giant multi-column footers copied from Apple or Linear look awful with few links. ([jain/crucial-ui-elements 7:57](https://youtu.be/gKM6b2EnW1k?t=477))
- Switch to a multi-column footer only when you need more than about five links. ([jain/crucial-ui-elements 8:37](https://youtu.be/gKM6b2EnW1k?t=517))
